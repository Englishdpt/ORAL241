# The 5-step analysis method (Terminale)

Every text in the app can carry a model analysis in these five steps. Students read it, then repeat it sentence by sentence.

| Step | Content | Typical phrases |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Introduction** | Title, author (end of text), source (newspaper, magazine, website, novel, biography…), date of publication, nature (article, poem, play, letter, song…), genre (informative, descriptive, narrative, argumentative, injunctive, expository…), general idea, writer's intention | “The text entitled…”, “…written by… / whose author is not mentioned…”, “…taken from…”, “…published on…”, “It is a press article.”, “This text deals with…”, “The author aims at showing…” |
| **2. Summary** | a) Text structure: parts with their line numbers. b) Summary proper: who, where, when, what, why, how | “The text can be divided into three parts. The first part is from line 1 to line… and is about…” |
| **3. Commentary** | 1 to 3 aspects, explained and justified by quotations: characters, setting, action, style (figures of speech), tone | “The text is built on a contrast between… and…” |
| **4. Evaluation** | a) Value of the text: interest (social, political, scientific, cultural, didactic…), what you learnt, the author's opinion. b) Personal opinion: agree or disagree, and why | “I agree with the writer because…” |
| **5. Branching out** | Use the text as a trampoline: link an idea to your own environment or experience (contextualization) | “This text reminds me of…”, “This text makes me think of…”, “After reading this text, I think of…” |

## How to store one analysis

```json
[
  {"title": "1. Introduction",  "content": "The text entitled … is taken from … It is a press article. …"},
  {"title": "2. Summary",       "content": "The text can be divided into three parts. …"},
  {"title": "3. Commentary",    "content": "…"},
  {"title": "4. Evaluation",    "content": "…"},
  {"title": "5. Branching out", "content": "…"}
]
```

Write each step as plain spoken English, in full sentences. Avoid brackets and abbreviations, because the same text is read aloud by speech synthesis and then repeated by the student. Quotation marks are fine.
